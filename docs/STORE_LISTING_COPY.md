## App Store draft

### Subtitle

**Supplement Cycle Tracker**

### Promotional text

Build a supplement stack, follow complex routines, track adherence, and keep each profile separated. Pro adds advanced cycles, analytics, encrypted sync and export; Coach adds multi-client workflows.

### Description

OAK Healthy helps supplement-heavy users keep routines structured without turning a daily stack into a spreadsheet.

Track supplements, doses and reminder times. See what is due today, record Taken or Skipped actions, and review recent adherence history.

Free includes core tracking, reminders, one profile and 7 days of history.

OAK Pro adds advanced On/Off, weekday and interval scheduling, up to 90 days of history, adherence analytics, encrypted cloud sync and data export.

OAK Coach adds multi-client management, up to 365 days of history and coach-oriented workflows while keeping each profile's stack, history, notifications and sync ownership separated.

Subscription prices and available offers are shown by the App Store for the current storefront. Purchases are tied to the user's App Store account and can be restored from Plan & Access.

OAK Healthy is a wellness and routine-tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent disease and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

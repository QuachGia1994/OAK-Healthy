# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

OAK Healthy is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Respect different viewpoints and lived experiences.
- Accept responsibility, correct mistakes, and focus on the project and its
  users.
- Protect other people's privacy, especially health and security data.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, trolling, personal
attacks, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit,
remove, reject, or restrict contributions and participation that violate it.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method
on the [QuachGia1994 GitHub profile](https://github.com/QuachGia1994). Do not
publish sensitive details in an issue. Reports will be handled as privately and
fairly as possible.
